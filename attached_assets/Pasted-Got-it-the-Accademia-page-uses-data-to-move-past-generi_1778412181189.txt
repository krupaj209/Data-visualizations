## Questions people ask + what data makes the answer better

### 🕐 Timing questions

| Question | What data makes it better |
|---|---|
| **What's the best time of day to cruise?** | Crowd index by departure slot (10am vs 1pm vs 6pm) + light quality for photos |
| **Which days of the week are least crowded?** | Weekday vs weekend passenger volume — Saturdays are visibly busier but by how much? |
| **What time does the sunset cruise actually start seeing the golden hour?** | Sunset time by month mapped to departure times (changes from 4pm in Dec to 9pm in June) |
| **Is it worth going in winter?** | Daylight hours by month vs cruise duration — a 90-min cruise in December means 60% of it is dark |

---

### 🎟️ Booking & ticket questions

| Question | What data makes it better |
|---|---|
| **How far in advance do I need to book?** | Lead time by season — July/August slots sell out X days ahead vs October |
| **Is hop-on hop-off actually worth it vs a fixed sightseeing cruise?** | Average stops used per visitor + time between boats at each pier |
| **Which ticket type gives the best value?** | Price-per-minute of actual cruise time across ticket types |
| **Are dinner cruises worth the price premium?** | Price delta vs standalone cruise + meal, alongside review sentiment |

---

### 🗺️ Boarding & logistics questions

| Question | What data makes it better |
|---|---|
| **Which pier should I board from?** | Walk time from top landmarks (Tower Bridge, London Eye, St Paul's) to each pier |
| **How long is the queue at Westminster Pier?** | Wait time by time of day — worst between 11am–1pm |
| **How long does the full route actually take?** | Actual journey time Westminster → Greenwich vs the advertised time |

---

### ⛅ Experience questions

| Question | What data makes it better |
|---|---|
| **When do I get the best photography conditions?** | Golden hour by month + which side of the boat faces which landmarks |
| **Is it worth doing on a cloudy/rainy day?** | % of days with rain per month + whether the boats are covered |
| **How many landmarks do I actually pass?** | Landmark count by route, mapped to approximate minute-marks in the journey |
